Tracking should reduce stress, not create more of it.

## Use one pipeline

Stick to a few clear states:

1. Saved
2. Applied
3. Interviewing
4. Offer / Rejected

## Keep notes lightweight

For each role, save:

- Why this role matters
- What to revise in your resume
- Next follow-up date

## Review weekly

A short weekly review helps you:

- Spot stalled applications
- Improve tailoring quality
- Focus on high-fit roles
