A great resume is mostly about **clarity** and **signal density**.

## 1. Lead with outcomes, not tasks

Instead of:

- Built internal dashboard

Write:

- Built an internal dashboard used by 5 teams, reducing incident triage time by 38%.

## 2. Keep formatting predictable

ATS parsers and human readers both benefit from stable structure:

- One-column layout
- Clear section headings
- Consistent date/location formatting

## 3. Tailor before every serious application

Pick a target role, scan the job description, and align:

- Keywords
- Tooling stack
- Business context

Small tailoring loops usually outperform mass-applying with one generic resume.
